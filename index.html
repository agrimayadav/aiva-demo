This is what a person sees the moment they ask HappySmile to contact them (website form, Instagram/Google ad or listing site).

AIVA is HappySmile's AI assistant, not a person. It can book a consultation, cancel a booking, and answer questions on timings, locations and prices. It doesn't give medical advice. The call is recorded to book your visit; AIVA will ask for your OK first. Available 9 AM–8 PM.
Demo script: things to try
  • Happy path: "yes" → "braces" → "Koramangala" → "Friday 6 pm" → "Rahul"
  • Info then book: "yes" → "how much is an implant?" → "Whitefield" → "the second one" → "Anil"
  • Urgent (P0): "yes" → "my tooth is swollen and hurts" → AIVA won't book and flags urgent
  • Clinical (P1): "is root canal painful?" → no medical advice, handed to staff
  • Price talk (P1): "do you have EMI?" · Human (P2): "can I talk to a person"
  • No consent: answer "no" to the first question
  • Cancellation: "yes" → "I want to cancel my appointment" → "Sunita"
  • Hindi: pick हिंदी → "हाँ" → "ब्रेसेस" → "इंदिरानगर" → "शनिवार चार बजे" → "मेरा नाम अमित है"
  • Drop-off: start a call and press End call. It shows up as an early drop in the front-desk metrics.
