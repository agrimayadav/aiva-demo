Handoff rules

AIVA hands a call to the front desk when any rule matches. New rules go live after the front-desk lead approves them.

    How to use this demo

    This is the screen HappySmile's front desk would use. AIVA, the AI voice assistant, answers every enquiry call. Calls it can't finish come here for a person to follow up. All data is fictional.

    What you're looking at

    • Headline: how many people are waiting for a callback, split by priority. P0 call within 15 min, P1 within 1 hr, P2 today.
    • Needs a callback: calls AIVA handed to staff, most urgent first. Each card says why it was handed off.
    • Handled by AIVA: calls AIVA finished on its own: Booked Cancelled Closed (question answered). Staff only review these.

    Try these

    1. Click Transcript on the unknown P2 caller to read the full call.
    2. Click Booked by staff on a callback. The card leaves the queue and the headline count goes down. Click Undo to bring it back.
    3. Click Mark complete for a callback that was resolved without a booking.
    4. Rate any call 👍 or 👎. This tells the team whether AIVA handled it correctly.
    5. Open Handoff rules, type a new rule and click Save rule. It stays grey as Pending approval until the front-desk lead approves it.

    Good to know

    • Call and SMS open your device's phone or messaging app. The numbers are fictional.
    • Your clicks are saved only in your browser. Reset demo at the bottom restores the sample data.
    • "Booked by staff" is counted separately from AIVA bookings, so AIVA's success metric stays honest.
