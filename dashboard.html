Handoff rules owned by front desk

The front-desk team decides when AIVA must stop and pass the call to a person. AIVA never gives medical advice and never pretends to be human.
P0 Urgent: call within 15 minPain, swelling, bleeding, fever, broken tooth. AIVA doesn't book; it flags.
P1 Clinical or money: call today"Is it painful/safe?", medicine questions, EMI, discounts, insurance.
P2 Preference: call todayAsked for a person, said no to AI/recording, AIVA misunderstood 3×, or no suitable slot.
Low confidence booking (<70%)Booked, but staff double-check by phone.

Deliberately not tracked

No-show rate. It depends on reminders, clinic wait times and patients' plans, not on how fast or well AIVA answers. Tracking it in the pilot would blur whether AIVA works.
Not in v1: SMS/WhatsApp follow-up for people who hung up after hearing slots. We measure the gap first (see KPI) before building it.
